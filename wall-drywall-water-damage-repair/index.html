Wall and Drywall Water Damage Repair in Athens, OH

Drywall wicks water like a wick. Find how far it went, dry what can be dried and replace the rest.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Drywall is paper and gypsum, and it behaves badly when wet. Water climbs the wall by wicking, paint bubbles, baseboards swell and the bottom of the wall softens. This page covers drying and repairing walls, baseboards and the wall cavity after a leak or flood.

Wet ceilings are covered on the ceiling page. This page focuses on walls. The source of the water may be a pipe, a window, a roof or the floor below.

What water does to a wall

Water soaks into the bottom of the drywall and climbs through the paper and gypsum. It can rise several inches above the visible stain. It also reaches baseboards, insulation and the bottom plate of the wall framing.

Typical signs are a stain on the baseboard, paint that bubbles or peels, a wall that feels cool or soft to the touch, and a baseboard that swells or separates. A stain near a corner often means the water came from above or behind the wall.

Dry in place or cut it out?

Drywall that was wet briefly with clean water and still feels firm can often be dried in place. Crews remove baseboards, drill small holes at the base or lift a strip near the floor to let air flow in. Drywall that sags, crumbles or has been wet for days is cut out and replaced.

Insulation inside the wall is usually removed if it was wet, because it holds water against the drywall. Framing is dried and checked before new drywall goes in. If the water was contaminated, such as from sewage or floodwater, drywall is removed above the water line.

How crews dry and repair a wall

Crews use a moisture meter and a thermal camera to find the wet extent. Baseboards come off, small holes or cuts open the cavity and air movers and a dehumidifier dry the wall from the inside. Some jobs use directed airflow in the wall cavity.

After readings reach the goal, damaged drywall is cut out and replaced. New sections are taped, finished, primed with a stain-blocking primer and painted. Matching texture and paint takes care.

Water that came from a pipe behind the wall needs a plumber to fix the line. The burst pipe page covers the first steps.

Baseboards and trim

Baseboards and trim wick water from the floor and swell. Painted wood trim can often be dried and reused, but swollen trim usually needs to be replaced. Water damage on baseboards is often the first sign of a leak behind a wall or under a floor.

A stain along the bottom of a wall may mean the floor was wet, so check the flooring too. The hardwood floor page and the carpet page explain what happens there.

Older houses on the West Side often have plaster and lath that dries differently from drywall.

Under ANSI/IICRC S500, wall cavities are dried and checked with moisture readings before drywall is closed. Gypsum board manufacturers also advise that board that stays wet should be replaced.

Safety with wet walls

Keep these in mind while you wait for help.

  • Do not cut into a wall that may hold wiring or pipes.
  • Keep people out from under a bulging ceiling or wall.
  • Wear a dust mask if you sand or scrape old paint.

Why older Athens walls need a careful look

Older houses in Athens often have plaster walls or drywall over plaster, along with several layers of paint. Plaster dries slowly and can crack as it dries. Walls over crawl spaces and basements may be exposed to moisture from below.

Exterior walls and walls shared with a bathroom are common places for leaks. If you rent, tell your landlord and photograph the wall. Ohio landlords must keep plumbing in good and safe working order under Ohio Revised Code 5321.04, so a leak in a wall is part of their responsibility to address.

What affects the cost of wall and drywall repair

Replacing a section of drywall costs less than rebuilding a whole wall, so catching it early helps.

Frequently asked questions

Can wet drywall be saved?

Sometimes. Firm drywall that was wet briefly with clean water can often be dried in place. Soft or crumbling drywall is replaced.

Why did the paint bubble?

Water behind the paint lifts it. Bubbles usually mean the drywall is wet or was wet. A moisture meter confirms.

Do the baseboards have to come off?

Usually yes, so air can reach the bottom of the wall and the cavity can be checked.

Will the stain return after painting?

It can if the source is not fixed and the wall is not dry. Use a stain-blocking primer after drying.

Is a wet wall an emergency?

It is urgent if water is still entering. Call the line, which is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.
