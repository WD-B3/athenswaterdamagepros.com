How to Shut Off Your Water Main in Athens, OH Homes

The fastest way to limit water damage is to stop the water. Most people learn where the valve is only after the flood begins.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

By the Athens Water Damage Pros team · Published November 10, 2026

A burst supply line can release gallons a minute. Every minute you spend searching for the valve is another minute of water on your floors. Finding the main shutoff takes five minutes on a calm day, and it can save thousands of dollars on a bad one.

This guide shows where main shutoffs usually are, how to close them and what to do if the valve will not turn.

Where to look for the main shutoff

Basement or crawl space

The main line usually enters through the foundation wall near the front of the house. The valve is just inside, often near the meter or the water heater.

Utility room or garage

In slab houses the pipe may come up inside a utility closet, near the water heater or in an interior wall.

Outside by the meter

A meter box in the yard or near the street holds the utility's valve. This one often needs a special key. Do not touch the meter unless you know how.

Apartments and rentals

There may be a unit shutoff in a closet or under a sink. Ask the property manager where the building valve is.

If you live on a private well, the main shutoff is usually near the pressure tank. Turning off the well pump stops the supply.

Two kinds of valves

A gate valve has a round handle you turn clockwise many times to close. Over the years it can seize or leave the washer stuck. A ball valve has a lever handle. A quarter turn, so the lever sits across the pipe, closes it. Ball valves are easier in an emergency.

If you have a gate valve, turn it fully closed and then a bit back. Use steady pressure, not force. A seized valve can break.

Practice turning the valve once a year.

Step by step: shut it off in an emergency

  1. Find the main valve and turn it fully closed.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house and flush toilets to drain the pipes.
  3. Turn off the water heater (or set gas to pilot) so it does not run dry.
  4. Do not touch electrical equipment in or near the water.
  5. Take photos, then call the 24/7 line and describe where the water went.

If the leak is at a single fixture, close that fixture's shutoff instead. The water heater has its own shutoff on the cold inlet. The water heater leak page shows how to stop a failed tank.

Water running and no valve in sight? Call the 24/7 line.

The American Red Cross and FEMA both advise homeowners to learn where utility shutoffs are before an emergency.

If water is near electrical equipment

Athens County uses a county-wide Enhanced 911 system, so a call to 911 reaches dispatchers who know the county. If water is near the electrical panel, or you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911 and the utility from outside.

The City of Athens fire department was founded in 1830, became full-time in the 1930s and operates two stations. Crews in Chauncey, The Plains and the villages around them depend on township and village departments, so confirm your local number on a card by your phone.

If the valve is stuck or leaking

Do not force a stuck valve with a pipe wrench or hammer. If it will not close, try the meter valve outside or call the water utility. Many utilities will send someone to shut off the supply at the street in an emergency. Ask your water utility for the emergency number and save it in your phone.

If the main valve leaks at the handle, tighten the packing nut a quarter turn. If it still leaks, have a plumber replace it. A valve that fails in an emergency is worth replacing on a calm day.

Make it easy for the next person

  • Tag the main valve with a bright label.
  • Make sure nothing is stacked in front of it.
  • Show everyone in the house how to turn it.
  • Take a photo of the valve and keep it on your phone.
  • Replace old gate valves with ball valves.

A plumber can add shutoff valves to fixtures that lack them. That is a small job that turns a flood into a drip.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the main water shutoff in most Athens houses?

Usually in the basement or crawl space near the front foundation wall, or in a utility closet in slab houses. Outside, a meter box holds the utility valve.

Can I shut off the water at the meter myself?

The meter valve belongs to the utility. Use the shutoff inside your house first, and call the utility if you cannot reach it.

How do I turn off water if my valve is stuck?

Do not force it. Try the meter valve outside or call your water utility. A plumber can replace a stuck valve.

Should I turn off the water heater too?

Yes, if the main is off for a while. A tank that runs dry can be damaged, and electric elements can burn out.

What do I do after the water is off?

Take photos, move valuables and call the 24/7 line. Standing water and wet materials need to be dried quickly.
