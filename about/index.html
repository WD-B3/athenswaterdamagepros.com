About Athens Water Damage Pros

A local water damage line for Athens, Ohio, with clear scope and price before work starts.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Athens Water Damage Pros answers water damage calls for Athens, Ohio and the towns around it. When a pipe bursts or a basement floods, you should not have to search for a number. You call one line, describe what happened and get a plan.

The site exists to explain what water damage is, what the work involves and what affects the cost, so you can make a decision while the water is still running. The pages are written for homeowners, tenants and small business owners.

How a call works

  1. You call the 24/7 line at (740) 231-9132.
  2. You describe the source, the rooms affected and how long the water has been there.
  3. You get the next steps, including what to shut off and what to photograph.
  4. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.
  5. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

What this site does not do

The site does not publish prices, because every loss is different. It does not promise arrival times. It does not use forms, so the only way to reach a crew is to call. It does not show reviews, because it has not collected any and will not invent them.

Where to read first

If water is spreading now, start with the water extraction page. If you are comparing situations, the services overview lists every page. For nearby towns, the service area page links to each one.

Frequently asked questions

Is the line staffed at night?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

Do you serve businesses?

Yes. See the commercial water damage page for how crews work in occupied buildings.

Are the crews licensed and insured?

Licensing and insurance are confirmed with the contractor who takes your job and are not assumed. Before work starts, ask for proof of insurance and for any license that applies. Ohio licenses certain trades, such as electrical and plumbing, through the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board.

Water Spreading Right Now?

Call the 24/7 emergency line and describe what you see. Stop the source first if it is safe to do so.
