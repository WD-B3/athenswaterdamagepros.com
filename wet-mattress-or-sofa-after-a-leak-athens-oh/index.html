Wet Mattress or Sofa After a Leak in Athens, OH: Save It or Toss It?

The water source and the time since the leak decide it. Clean water caught fast is a different story from floodwater.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

By the Athens Water Damage Pros team · Published November 10, 2026

A ceiling leak soaks a bed. A pipe floods the living room and the sofa sits in an inch of water. What now? Soft furniture soaks up water and holds it, which makes it a problem long after the floor dries.

Whether to dry or discard depends on what the water was, how long the item stayed wet and what it is made of. This guide covers the decision for the pieces people ask about most.

Step one: what kind of water was it?

Water from a clean supply line, a sink or an overhead pipe is the least risky. Water from a washing machine, dishwasher or aquarium may carry more soap or bacteria. Water from a sewer, a toilet overflow or a flooded creek is contaminated.

Contaminated water makes porous items unsafe. Mattresses, upholstered furniture and carpet that touched sewage or floodwater are generally discarded. The sewage page explains why porous materials cannot be cleaned.

ItemClean water, caught fastContaminated or old water
Mattress and box springSometimes dried, often replaced if soaked throughDiscard
Upholstered sofaSometimes dried and cleanedDiscard
Solid wood furnitureUsually dried and savedOften cleaned if not porous
Pillows and cushionsWash and dry, or replaceDiscard
RugsOften dried and cleanedDiscard, or professional cleaning if valuable
Particleboard furnitureOften swells, may be discardedDiscard

These are general patterns. The item and the situation decide.

What to do in the first hour

  1. Stop the water and keep people off the wet item.
  2. Lift the item off wet carpet or floor. Put foil or blocks under the legs.
  3. Remove cushions and bedding, which dry faster apart.
  4. Blot with towels. Do not use a household vacuum on soaked fabric.
  5. Move air with fans and run a dehumidifier. Open windows when the weather allows.

Do not put a soaked mattress outside in the sun and expect it to dry. A mattress holds water deep in the foam and springs, and the interior stays damp. Open-air drying is also hard in a damp valley.

How long can furniture stay wet?

Dry within one to two days if you can. After that the risk of odor and microbial growth goes up quickly. If the item is still damp after two days, or if you smell mustiness, discard it or have it assessed.

The EPA advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit growth. That guidance applies to furniture, carpet and drywall alike.

The CDC publishes guidance on cleaning up after floods that says porous items soaked by contaminated water should generally be discarded.

Flood-soaked furniture near the creeks

Chauncey sits near the confluence of Sunday Creek and the Hocking River and has long been vulnerable to flooding. Furniture soaked by creek water carries silt and contaminants and is usually discarded rather than dried.

If water entered in Chauncey or in Amesville, where Federal Creek flooded in June 1998, take photos of each item before it goes to the curb. Your insurer will want a list with descriptions and approximate values.

Source type: Public reference (Wikipedia)

Mattresses: the hard case

Mattresses are the most difficult item. Foam and fiber hold water, and a soaked mattress takes days to dry in a warm room. If water came from a clean source, you caught it within hours and only the surface is wet, you can blot, press out water and dry it on its side with fans. If it is soaked through, replacement is usually the practical answer.

Ask your insurer whether your policy covers contents. Keep the item or a photo until the adjuster says otherwise.

Sofas and upholstered chairs

Upholstery over a wood frame can sometimes be saved. Crews or a professional upholstery cleaner remove the cushions, extract water, treat the fabric and dry the foam. If the frame is particleboard or the fabric stains, replacement may cost less than cleaning.

Leather can warp and crack when wet. Ask a leather specialist before treating it yourself.

What to do with items you must discard

  • Photograph each item and note the brand and age.
  • Keep a small swatch or a tag as proof, if the adjuster wants it.
  • Wrap heavy, wet items in plastic so they do not drip on the way out.
  • Ask your trash hauler how to dispose of bulky items.
  • Add the item to your inventory list for the claim.

Frequently asked questions

Can a wet mattress be saved?

Sometimes, if the water was clean, the leak was brief and only the surface is wet. A mattress soaked through is usually replaced.

How fast do I need to dry a wet couch?

Within a day or two. Past 48 hours, odor and growth become more likely.

Is it safe to keep furniture that touched floodwater?

Porous furniture that touched floodwater or sewage is generally discarded. Hard, non-porous pieces may be cleaned.

Will insurance replace a ruined mattress?

It may, if the policy covers the cause. Contents coverage varies. Ask your insurer and keep photos.

Can I dry a mattress in the sun?

The surface may dry, but the inside stays damp. Do not rely on sun alone.
