AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Athens, OH

A clogged condensate line can drip for weeks. Dry the water, fix the drain and repair what got wet.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

An air conditioner makes water every time it runs. If the drain line clogs, the pan overflows and that water goes into the ceiling, the closet or the attic. This page covers the damage that results from condensate leaks, from stained drywall to wet insulation and soft flooring.

The unit itself, the drain line and the float switch are HVAC work. Restoration covers drying and repair. If the leak is already staining a ceiling right now, the ceiling page explains the urgent steps.

How an AC leak soaks a house

Cooling air removes moisture from the air, and that moisture drips into a pan and drains through a pipe. When algae or debris blocks the pipe, the pan fills and overflows. Air handlers in attics and closets over finished rooms are the usual trouble spots, because the overflow drips onto a ceiling or runs into a wall.

A slow leak shows up as a brown ring on a ceiling or a damp spot on the carpet below. Because it runs only when the system is on, it often goes unnoticed through a whole cooling season. Window units and mini-split systems can drip down the wall into baseboards and flooring.

Signs to check before it gets worse

  • A stain or soft spot on the ceiling below the air handler.
  • Water in the secondary drain pan or around the unit.
  • A damp or musty smell in a closet near the system.
  • An air conditioner that stops running when the float switch trips.
  • Baseboards and carpet that stay damp near an indoor unit.

How crews dry and repair AC leak damage

Before the drying starts, a technician should clear the line. Otherwise the pan overflows again and the work is wasted. Crews then check the ceiling cavity, insulation and framing with a moisture meter and thermal camera.

Wet insulation is removed and the cavity is dried with air movers and a dehumidifier. Drywall is patched or replaced once readings are normal. Because the water is clean condensate, many materials can be dried in place, but a leak that has run for weeks can leave staining and a musty smell in insulation and framing.

If the leak ran long enough that carpet and pad are damp, the carpet water damage page covers what can be saved.

Drying work on a wet ceiling or wall is commonly guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which defines how moisture is measured and when a structure counts as dry. Citing the standard describes the industry method and is not a claim that any person holds a certification.

Safety before you dry

Keep these in mind while you wait for help.

  • Turn off the air conditioner before touching a wet drain pan or ceiling.
  • Keep people out from under a sagging ceiling.
  • Do not use a wet vacuum near wet outlets or fixtures.

Why the damage matters even when the water is clean

Condensate water is clean to start with, so the first reaction is often to wipe up and move on. The trouble is time. A leak that runs through a summer soaks insulation and wood, and damp materials hold moisture long after the water stops. That is when ceilings stain, drywall softens and the closet smells.

Fixing the drain and drying the structure are two separate jobs, and each needs to be done. Check your system's drain line each spring, and ask an HVAC contractor to install a float switch if it does not have one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know the AC caused the stain?

The stain sits below or near the air handler and grows when the system runs. A moisture reading near the unit and water in the drain pan point to the system.

Can I clear the condensate line myself?

Some homeowners flush it with a wet vacuum or a mild cleaner, but HVAC contractors do this routinely and check the float switch at the same time. It is worth having done right.

Is condensate water dangerous?

It starts clean, but it can pick up algae and debris in the pan, and it can leave odor if it soaks insulation. Wear gloves for cleanup.

Will the stain come back after painting?

It can if the cavity was not dried or the source was not fixed. Use a stain-blocking primer after the structure is dry.

Does insurance cover an AC drain leak?

Sometimes, depending on whether it was sudden or gradual and on your policy language. Ask your insurer before filing.
