Service Area: Athens, OH and Nearby Towns

Home base is Athens. Crews also respond to the valley towns to the north, northwest and northeast.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Athens sits on the Hocking River in Athens County, and the nearest towns follow the river and the state routes out of the city. Crews respond across the city and to the five towns below. Each has its own page with local roads, creeks and housing.

If your address is nearby but not on the list, call the 24/7 line and give the street address. Calls from other parts of Athens County and neighboring areas are handled case by case. Plain mentions of Shade, Albany, Glouster, Coolville and Pomeroy are on this page only because callers from those places ask. They do not have separate pages.

Towns with their own pages

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve every town in Athens County?

Crews serve Athens and the five towns listed here. For other places in the county, call and ask. Availability depends on where crews are at the time.

How do I know if my address is covered?

Call the 24/7 line and give the street address. The dispatcher will confirm coverage and what to expect.

Neighborhoods inside Athens

The city has its own neighborhood pages, based on how the City of Athens describes them.

Near East Side, Athens

Brick streets, 1930s homes and a lot of low ground near the Hocking River. This is how water damage gets handled there.

South Side, Athens

Hillside homes, Richland Avenue and a creek that rose fast in August 2026. Here is what water damage looks like on this side of town.

West Side, Athens

Small lots, older houses and a mix of owners and renters. Most water damage here starts inside the walls.

North Side, Athens

Steep slopes, mature trees and homes of many ages. Roofs, gutters and ceilings are where this neighborhood often shows water first.

Far East Side, Athens

Single-family streets north of East State Street, with 1920s additions and post-war houses. This is how water damage gets handled there.

Uptown, Athens

Historic storefronts with apartments above, shared walls and student renters. Water in one unit often reaches the next.

Nearby towns

The Plains, OH

ZIP code 45780. The Plains sits just northwest of Athens. When a pipe fails or a storm pushes water in, one call starts the dry-out.

Chauncey, OH

ZIP code 45719. Chauncey sits near the meeting point of Sunday Creek and the Hocking River. Creek water and pipe failures both get the same fast response.

Nelsonville, OH

ZIP code 45764. Brick storefronts, older homes and busy streets: Nelsonville gets crews that dry downtown buildings and houses alike.

Millfield, OH

ZIP code 45761. Rural homes in Millfield face freeze-ups in winter and storm leaks in summer. Crews respond across the seasons.

Amesville, OH

ZIP code 45711. Amesville sits on Federal Creek, and its 1998 flood still shapes how neighbors think about water. Crews respond to creek water and plumbing alike.

Calls also come from Shade, Albany, Glouster, Coolville and Pomeroy. Call and ask whether your address can be reached.

Water Spreading Right Now?

Call the 24/7 emergency line and describe what you see. Stop the source first if it is safe to do so.
