Water Damage Restoration in the West Side, Athens, OH

Small lots, older houses and a mix of owners and renters. Most water damage here starts inside the walls.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132
45701

The West Side is, according to the City of Athens, the oldest neighborhood in town. It lies west of the center, with West State Street and West Union Street on its southern edge. Water damage here is mostly a story about older houses on small lots: a leaking supply line, a water heater at the end of its life, a bathroom that overflowed or a roof that let rain in.

How the city describes the West Side

The Athens 2040 plan calls the West Side the oldest neighborhood in Athens, with modest older homes on small lots and a mix of owner-occupied and rental housing. It names ARTS/West and West State Street Park as anchors, and notes that University Estates, a large suburban development, sits at the far west end.

An earlier city neighborhood document describes mostly low-density single-family housing with apartments on the edges and some student housing, and notes that part of the northwest area lies in a wellhead buffer zone that limits development.

What the sources do and do not say

The city plan also labels this area Camp Wool. Neither the plan nor the earlier neighborhood document mentions flooding in the West Side, so this page makes no flood claim.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. If you are buying or insuring a West Side house, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for the exact address.

The plan does not report how many homes have basements or crawl spaces, so this page does not either.

Common situations in older houses

Supply line and fixture leaks

Braided washer hoses, toilet fittings and sink connections fail slowly or all at once. The appliance leak page lists what to shut off first.

Water heater failures

Tanks wear out. A leak can flood a utility room or a basement. See the water heater leak page.

Wet drywall and plaster

Water travels inside wall cavities. The wall and drywall page explains when board can be dried and when it must go.

Rental problems

Tenants should shut off the valve, photograph the damage and tell the landlord in writing. See the carpet page for wet floors.

What a first visit looks like

A crew traces the water to its source and uses a moisture meter and a thermal camera to find wet areas that look dry. They explain the scope and price before removing anything. Extraction comes first, then removal of what cannot be saved, then drying with air movers and dehumidifiers until readings reach a dry standard.

The water extraction page covers the first stage in more detail.

Insurance and landlords

Renters should ask their own insurer about contents coverage and give the landlord written notice. Owners can use photos and moisture readings to describe what happened. Coverage depends on the cause and the policy, so ask the insurer rather than assuming.

Ohio landlords must keep plumbing and supplied appliances in good working order under Ohio Revised Code 5321.04, which is useful to know when a leak starts in a rental.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code on the West Side?

The West Side is in ZIP code 45701. For addresses just outside the city, call and give the street address so the dispatcher can confirm coverage.

Is emergency water removal available on the West Side?

The emergency line is answered 24/7. Arrival time depends on where crews are at the moment, so describe the damage and ask what to expect.

I rent my house. Who should I call first?

Close the valve if you can, photograph the damage and tell the landlord in writing. Then call the line so drying can start.

Is the West Side in a flood zone?

Neither city source reviewed for this page mentions flooding there. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your exact address.

Can wet plaster be saved?

Sometimes, if it is dried quickly and readings reach a dry standard. A crew decides after taking moisture readings.
