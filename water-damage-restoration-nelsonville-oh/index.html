Brick storefronts, older homes and busy streets: Nelsonville gets crews that dry downtown buildings and houses alike.

Nelsonville is the largest town near Athens, with a historic downtown, a college and neighborhoods that range from older homes to newer builds. Crews serving ZIP code 45764 respond to leaks and floods in houses, apartments and storefronts, and they bring the same drying equipment to a Public Square building that they bring to a ranch house.

Downtown buildings and Public Square

Nelsonville's central business district is on the National Register of Historic Places, and the Public Square is paved with Star Bricks. Older masonry buildings often have flat roofs, shared walls and plumbing that runs through several units. A leak in one unit can reach the next.

When water reaches a commercial space, the priority is stopping it, protecting inventory and getting the area open again. The commercial water damage page explains how crews work in occupied buildings.

What is specific to Nelsonville

Nelsonville was settled in 1814 by Daniel Nelson and incorporated in 1838. The Hocking River runs along the city and Monday Creek flows through its eastern part. Canal Street lies over the former Hocking Canal. The city sits at an elevation of about 702 feet in York Township.

Stuart's Opera House, built in 1879, is described as Ohio's last fully operating second-story opera house. The Nelsonville Brick Company operated from 1877 to 1937, and its Nelsonville Block won first prize at the 1904 St. Louis World's Fair. Brick Kiln Park sits on the former company grounds.

US 33 runs through the city, and its bypass was completed in December 2013. The Hockhocking Adena Bikeway runs 22 miles to Athens. Nelsonville is home to Hocking College and the Nelsonville-York City School District, and Wayne National Forest surrounds the city. For flood risk at your address, check the FEMA flood map.

Students, tenants and landlords

Nelsonville has apartments and rented houses near the college and downtown. Tenants who find a leak should shut off the valve if they can, photograph the damage and tell the landlord in writing. Ohio landlords must keep plumbing and supplied appliances in good and safe working order under Ohio Revised Code 5321.04.

Safety in older buildings

Keep these in mind while you wait for help.

  • Do not disturb old plaster, insulation or flooring you cannot identify.
  • Stay out of rooms with water near the electrical panel.
  • Tell the crew the building age when you call.

Older houses and what they hide

Older houses in Nelsonville may have plaster over lath, original hardwood under carpet and plumbing that has been patched over time. If you find a stain, a swollen baseboard or a soft floor, ask a crew to take moisture readings before repairing. Painting over a wet wall hides the problem for a while.

If water is spreading in your Nelsonville building

Do these while you wait.

1

Stop the source

Close the shutoff for the fixture or the main valve for the building.

2

Protect electrical safety

Do not stand in water near outlets. Call the utility if the panel is wet.

3

Move contents up

Lift inventory, files and electronics off the floor.

4

Tell the property manager

If you do not own the building, notify the owner or manager right away.

5

Call the 24/7 line

Give the address, the source and which floors are wet.

Storm season in the Hocking Valley

Heavy rain tests roofs, gutters and drains. A gutter that overflows next to a foundation sends water into the basement. After a storm, check ceilings and upper walls for stains. The storm damage page covers wind and rain damage in detail.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Nelsonville?

Nelsonville is served as ZIP code 45764. For addresses outside the city limits, call and give the street address so crews can confirm coverage.

Is emergency water removal available in Nelsonville?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available, including nights and weekends.

Can you work in a downtown storefront while it is open?

Often yes. Crews can contain the wet area and dry it while the rest of the space stays in use. Describe the layout when you call.

Who pays if my apartment leaks into the unit below?

It depends on the cause and the lease. Document the damage, notify the landlord in writing and contact your renters insurer.

How do I find out whether my building is in a flood zone?

Check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer whether flood coverage applies.
