Emergency Burst Pipe Water Damage in Athens, OH

A burst supply line sprays water at full pressure. Shut off the water, then call the 24/7 line to start cleanup and drying.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

A burst pipe is the fastest way to flood a home, so this page assumes the water is running right now. Close the main water valve first, usually where the line enters near the meter, and then call the 24/7 emergency line. Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain what is left in the pipes.

A supply line carries water at household pressure, which is far more than a drain does. A split copper or plastic line can pour out hundreds of gallons in an hour. This page covers pressurized supply-line failures. Pipes that split because ice blocked them have their own page on frozen pipe damage.

What to do in the first ten minutes

  1. Shut off the main water valve. If it will not turn or cannot be found, call the Athens water utility and the emergency line at the same time.
  2. If water is near the electrical panel, outlets or a ceiling light fixture, stay out of the area and call the electric utility or 911 before anything else.
  3. Open the faucets, starting on the highest floor, to relieve pressure and drain the line.
  4. Move electronics, papers and small furniture out of the wet area. Lift rugs off the floor.
  5. Take photos and a short video of the break, the spray pattern and the wet rooms.

Where burst pipes usually fail

A burst supply line rarely fails in the middle of a clean run. It fails at a fitting, a solder joint, a worn compression connection or a spot weakened by corrosion. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated spaces and under sinks take the brunt, and older galvanized or copper lines near the end of their service life fail more often than newer plastic ones.

In Athens, many houses near downtown and along East State Street were built long before modern plumbing, and some still have mixed generations of piping. A leak under a kitchen sink is the same kind of failure, and the kitchen water damage page covers cabinets and the sink area.

How cleanup and restoration work after a burst pipe

Crews start by confirming the water is off and mapping where it traveled. A thermal camera and moisture meter find water inside walls and under floors. A pipe that burst in an upstairs wall often soaked the ceiling below, so both floors are checked.

Standing water is extracted, then wet insulation, soaked drywall at the base of walls and saturated pad are removed. Air movers and dehumidifiers are set to dry what remains. Because supply-line water starts clean, more materials can often be dried in place than after sewage or flood water, provided the work starts quickly.

Repair is the last step. Drywall, paint, trim and flooring are replaced once the readings show the structure is dry. The pipe itself is a plumber's job, so line up a plumber while the restoration crew dries.

Why speed matters more than the size of the leak

Drywall and insulation hold water like a sponge. The first day determines how much of it has to be thrown away. Drywall left wet for a few days can swell and crumble, and flooring starts to cup or lift as boards absorb moisture.

That is also why a pipe that burst while the house was empty is more costly than one caught right away. If you come home to water, do not wait until morning to call.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration classifies water by source and by how much moisture materials absorb. A supply line that bursts is normally a clean-water source at first, which is why speed matters before it picks up contamination.

Burst pipes and renters in Athens

About three in four occupied homes in Athens are rented, so many burst pipes happen in a unit someone does not own. Under Ohio Revised Code 5321.04, a landlord must keep plumbing fixtures in good and safe working order and supply running water. Tell your landlord at once and get the response in writing, and photograph your belongings for your renter's insurance.

Do not authorize restoration work in a rental without your landlord's approval. If the landlord cannot be reached, call the line and explain the situation.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Athens?

Yes, any hour. A crew can be sent the same day when one is available. If you cannot reach the main shutoff, say so on the call so the dispatcher can talk you through the next step.

Where is the main water shutoff in my house?

Usually where the water line enters the building, near the meter or the water heater, in a basement, crawl space or utility room. It is a round handle or a lever. If you cannot find it, the water utility can shut off service at the street.

Should I turn off the power if water is near outlets?

Only if you can reach the panel while standing on a dry surface. If the panel is wet or you would have to stand in water, stay away and call the electric utility or 911.

Can the damaged drywall be dried instead of replaced?

Sometimes. Drywall that was wet briefly with clean water and still holds its shape can often be dried in place. Drywall that sags, crumbles or stays wet behind paint is usually cut out and replaced. The wall and drywall page shows how crews decide.

Do you also repair the pipe?

The line itself is a plumbing repair. Restoration handles water removal, drying and the rebuild afterward, so call a plumber at the same time and tell the crew when the leak is fixed.
