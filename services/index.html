Water Damage Restoration Services in Athens, OH

Pick the situation that matches yours. Each page explains the cause, the process and what affects cost.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Water damage does not look the same twice. A frozen pipe, a failed water heater and a creek that overtopped its banks all call for different first steps. The pages below are grouped by cause and by the part of the home that got wet, so you can find the one that fits.

Every service starts the same way. The source is stopped, standing water is removed, wet materials are assessed and the structure is dried until moisture readings reach a dry standard. If you are not sure which page applies, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.

Not sure which service you need? Call and describe the damage.

Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A burst supply line sprays water at full pressure. Shut off the water, then call the 24/7 line to start cleanup and drying.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Ice often hides the damage until the thaw. When the pipe starts leaking, shut off the water and call the 24/7 line.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

A failed tank can release dozens of gallons at once. Shut off the supply and power, then call the 24/7 line.

Slab Leak Water Damage

A pipe leaking under a concrete floor is hidden, but its damage is not. Call the 24/7 line when you see signs.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

A washer hose that lets go can empty into a laundry room in minutes. Shut off the supply valve and call the 24/7 line.

AC Leak Water Damage

A clogged condensate line can drip for weeks. Dry the water, fix the drain and repair what got wet.

Kitchen Water Damage

Kitchens hide water in cabinets, under floors and behind appliances. Find it, dry it and repair it.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

A sagging ceiling can drop without warning. Keep people out from under it and call the 24/7 line.

Storm Water Damage

Storm damage lets water in from above and the side. Make it safe, protect belongings and call the 24/7 line.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Floodwater from the Hocking River, a creek or runoff is treated as contaminated. Stay out of it and call the 24/7 line.

Basement Water Damage

A wet basement damages stored belongings and finished walls fast. Stay out of deep water and call the 24/7 line.

Crawl Space Water Damage

A wet crawl space soaks joists, insulation and the floor above. Pump it, dry it and fix what let the water in.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

Standing water ruins flooring and drywall quickly. Call the 24/7 line and crews can start pumping and extracting as soon as they are on site.

Structural Drying

Removing water is only half of the job. Drying finishes it, and the readings say when it is done.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Sewage is contaminated water. Keep people and pets away, stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Hardwood absorbs water and moves. Dry it quickly and the boards often recover. Wait, and they cup or buckle.

Carpet Water Damage

Carpet and the pad beneath it are separate materials with separate fates. Extract fast and the carpet can often be saved.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Drywall wicks water like a wick. Find how far it went, dry what can be dried and replace the rest.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Every hour a business is closed costs money. Call the 24/7 line to start extraction and drying.
